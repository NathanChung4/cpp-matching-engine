# A guided tour of price-time priority. Run: build/release/me ladder scenarios/walkthrough.txt
# Prices are dollars with 2 decimals; quantities are shares. Each line is one order.

# 1-4. Build a book: two asks at 100.05 (#1 arrived first), one at 100.07, one bid.
limit sell 1 100.05 10
limit sell 2 100.05 5
limit sell 3 100.07 20
limit buy  4 100.00 15

# 5. A buy at 100.05 for 12: takes all of #1 (first in line), then 2 of #2. Partial fill.
limit buy  5 100.05 12

# 6. A buy that crosses two levels: 3 left at 100.05, then 100.07. The rest (7) rests at 100.07.
limit buy  6 100.07 30

# 7. A market sell sweeps the bids from the best down; what is left over expires.
limit buy  7 99.98 10
market sell 8 40

# 8. Cancel a resting order, then try to cancel one that already filled (#1).
limit sell 9 100.10 8
cancel 9
cancel 1

# 9. Modify loses queue priority: #10 was first at 100.20, after the modify #11 is first.
limit sell 10 100.20 5
limit sell 11 100.20 5
modify 10 100.20 5
limit buy  12 100.20 5

# 10. IOC takes what it can and never rests; FOK is all or nothing.
ioc buy 13 100.25 9
fok buy 14 100.30 50
limit sell 15 100.30 40
fok buy 16 100.30 40
